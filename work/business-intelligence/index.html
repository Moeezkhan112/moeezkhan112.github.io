Case study 01 / Full Stack · Business Intelligence

A clearer picture of the business.

An operational CRM becomes a decision-making tool. Sales, commissions and agent performance, connected in one platform.

Project

Bitrix24 BI Platform

My role

End-to-end product development, CRM integration and business logic

  • Bitrix24 API
  • Data modelling
  • Role-based access
  • Historical caching

01 / The challenge

Start with the problem.

Management depended on an administrator to enter KPI data into a standalone dashboard. Every update required manual work before the business could see its performance.

02 / The approach

Connect the moving parts.

  1. 01

    Connected the platform directly to operational Bitrix24 data.

  2. 02

    Built reporting across sales, leads, listings, departments and individual agents.

  3. 03

    Implemented commission allocation, gross profit, agent net, company net and performance grading.

Bitrix24 data
→Business rules
→Performance insights

03 / Engineering decisions

The thinking behind the work.

Business rules before charts

Modelled attribution and commission rules so the numbers reflect how the business operates.

Access with context

Added role-based access for the people using the platform.

A view over time

Used historical caching to support KPI trends alongside operational reporting.

04 / The outcome

What changed.

Evolved a manually maintained dashboard into an API-driven business intelligence platform that supports day-to-day management decisions.
